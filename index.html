House of Maratha: privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

House of Maratha is a single-player strategy game. It runs entirely on your device.

We collect nothing. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Permissions. The app lists only Android's standard internet permission, which is included by default in apps built with a web engine. The game itself never connects to the internet, and it plays fully offline.

Your saved game is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. If you use Android's own device backup, your phone may include the save in your personal Google backup, which we cannot see. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes the save from your phone.

Children. The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If a future version ever changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.
